⚡ Protein Priority
At 51, muscle protein synthesis needs 0.9–1.0g per lb (~170g/day). Spread across 4+ meals. Post-workout: 30g within 45 min. Before bed: cottage cheese or Greek yogurt (casein).
🕐 30-Min Session Fueling
Short, intense sessions benefit most from pre-workout carbs: 30–40g carbs + 15g protein 60 min before. This maximizes output. Short sessions also don't spike cortisol like 90-min grinds do — a direct advantage for belly fat loss.
🚫 Belly Fat — The Real Drivers
Visceral fat at 51 is driven by insulin spikes + cortisol. The top 3 offenders: alcohol, added sugar, refined carbs. Eliminate or drastically reduce these — nutrition does 70% of the work on belly fat.
💧 Hydration
100–120oz water/day. Add electrolytes on row days. Mild dehydration impairs fat oxidation and drops testosterone measurably.
💊 Supplement Stack
Creatine 5g/day — best-studied muscle preserver in men 50+. Vitamin D3+K2 — most men your age are deficient; impacts testosterone. Magnesium glycinate — sleep + recovery. Omega-3 2g EPA+DHA — inflammation reduction. Always check with your physician.

LOGGING A WORKOUT — STEP BY STEP
1.
Open Schedule tab and tap the day card for today's session (e.g. Monday = Upper Strength).
2.
Read the session description and check the phase-adjusted sets/reps shown for each exercise (these automatically increase as you progress through the 12 weeks).
3.
Do the exercise. After completing Set 1, come back to the modal. Enter the weight you used and the reps you completed, then tap LOG. The button turns lime to confirm.
4.
Repeat for each set and each exercise. You don't need to log every set — but the more you log, the better your Progress tab becomes for tracking strength gains.
5.
When the full session is done, tap MARK SESSION COMPLETE. The day card turns faded with a ✓, your streak counter increments, and the week completion bar updates.
PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD — THE KEY TO EVERYTHING
The sets and reps in the workout modal automatically increase as you advance through phases. Phase 1 has you doing 3×10–12 to learn the movements. Phase 4 pushes you to 5×6–8 at maximum weight. The program does the programming for you — your job is to add weight whenever the reps feel easy.

Rule of thumb: if you can complete all reps with 2+ reps left in the tank, increase the weight by 5 lbs next session. Track this in the Set Log — looking back at Week 1 weights vs Week 12 weights is one of the most motivating things you can do.
P90X AND 7MW — HOW THEY FIT
P90X
  • Ab Ripper X — Mon & Sat finisher. 15 min, 339 reps, no equipment. Do it right after lifting.
  • Kenpo X — Tuesday option. Swap for core finisher on high-energy days. Do 20 min minimum.
  • Yoga X — Wednesday recovery. The full 90 min is ideal but 45 min is fine. Don't skip this entirely.
7 MINUTE WORKOUT
  • Thursday warm-up — run one full circuit before lifting to activate the legs and raise heart rate.
  • Sunday optional — one round only if you feel genuinely good. Skip without any guilt if not.
  • Any morning — 7MW makes an excellent alarm-clock starter before work if you have 7 minutes to spare.
COMMON QUESTIONS
What if I miss a day?
Don't try to make it up — just continue from where you left off. Missing one session doesn't break the program. Missing two in a row is a pattern worth addressing. Missing three means life happened and that's fine — just restart the week.
Do I need all the equipment from day one?
No. The building gym covers Mon/Thu/Sat. Your apartment weights and rower cover the rest. P90X and 7MW need zero equipment. Start with what you have — the program is designed around your actual setup.
What happens after 12 weeks?
Take a deload week (lighter weights, same movements, 3 days only). Then restart the program at Week 1 with heavier starting weights than last time. The structure stays the same — your baseline has shifted. Most of the visible results actually compound in the second cycle.
The 30 minutes feels easy — should I do more?
Add weight, not time. A 30-minute session at high intensity with 45-second rest periods is genuinely demanding. If it feels easy, you're resting too long or lifting too light. Tighten the rest periods first, then increase weight.
My data is gone — what happened?
All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. It persists between sessions but is tied to the specific browser on the specific device. If you clear browser storage or use a different browser, data won't transfer. To back it up: Progress tab → copy the workout log data manually.
