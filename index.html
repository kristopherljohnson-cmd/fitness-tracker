⚡ Protein Priority
At 51, muscle protein synthesis needs 0.9–1.0g per lb (~170g/day). Spread across 4+ meals. Post-workout: 30g within 45 min.
🕐 30-Min Session Fueling
Short sessions benefit most from pre-workout carbs: 30–40g carbs + 15g protein 60 min before.
🚫 Belly Fat — The Real Drivers
Visceral fat is driven by insulin spikes + cortisol. Top offenders: alcohol, added sugar, refined carbs.
💧 Hydration
100–120oz water/day. Add electrolytes on row days.
💊 Supplement Stack
Creatine 5g/day — best-studied muscle preserver. Vitamin D3+K2. Magnesium glycinate. Omega-3 2g EPA+DHA.
